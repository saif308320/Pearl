Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Pearl & Co. Dental.

Are your dental procedures painful?

We pride ourselves on our 100% pain-free commitment. We use advanced local anesthesia techniques, soothing sedation options, and ultra-gentle tools to ensure total comfort.

Do you accept walk-ins or emergencies?

Yes! We prioritize dental emergencies and offer same-day urgent care appointments for severe pain, chipped teeth, or infections.

How often should I visit for a checkup?

We generally recommend routine dental exams and professional cleanings every 6 months to prevent plaque build-up and catch cavities early.
